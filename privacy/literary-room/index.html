Literary Room

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Literary Room ("Literary Room", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Literary Room mobile application (the "App").

Literary Room is a reading application that provides access to classic books and timeless literature.

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address to use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Literary Room does not directly collect or require users to provide personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Camera data
  • Microphone recordings
  • Precise location

However, certain third-party services integrated into the App may automatically process certain technical or device-related information as described below.

2. Advertising

Literary Room uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain information, which may include:

  • IP address
  • Advertising ID
  • App or device identifiers
  • Device information
  • Information about interactions with the App and advertisements
  • Diagnostic and performance information

This information may be used for purposes including:

  • Providing advertisements
  • Measuring advertising performance
  • Preventing fraud and abuse
  • Improving advertising services
  • Providing personalized or non-personalized advertising, depending on applicable settings and user choices

The information collected and processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's own privacy policies and applicable terms.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please refer to Google's privacy documentation.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

3. Push Notifications

Literary Room uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to deliver push notifications.

If you enable notifications, Firebase may process a device or messaging token and related technical information necessary to deliver notifications to your device.

Notifications may be used to inform you about:

  • New books
  • New content
  • App updates
  • Other information related to Literary Room

You can disable push notifications at any time through your device settings.

4. In-App Purchases

Literary Room offers an optional Premium purchase that removes advertisements from the App.

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing.

Literary Room does not receive or store your credit card number, debit card number, bank account information, or other payment credentials.

Payment processing is handled by Google Play according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Literary Room may process information necessary to verify and provide your purchased Premium entitlement, such as the purchase product and purchase transaction information provided through Google Play Billing.

5. How We Use Information

Information processed in connection with Literary Room may be used to:

  • Provide and operate the App
  • Deliver push notifications
  • Display advertisements
  • Measure and improve advertising performance
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Verify Premium purchases
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the App
  • Diagnose technical problems related to supported services

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Information Sharing

Literary Room does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with third-party service providers that are necessary to operate the App, including:

  • Google AdMob, for advertising services
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging, for push notifications
  • Google Play, for processing in-app purchases

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or property of Literary Room, our users, or others.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Literary Room does not require user accounts and therefore does not maintain account profiles containing information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained by those providers according to their respective privacy policies, retention practices, and legal obligations.

Information related to Premium purchases may be retained as necessary to verify and provide purchased functionality and to comply with applicable legal and financial requirements.

Because Literary Room does not provide user accounts, there is no Literary Room account that users need to delete.

If you have a privacy-related request, you may contact us using the information provided below.

8. Children's Privacy

Literary Room is not specifically directed to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the situation and take appropriate action where required by applicable law.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the App.

Information transmitted between the App and applicable third-party services is protected using appropriate security technologies, including encryption in transit where supported by the relevant service.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You may control certain privacy-related settings through your Android device and Google services.

For example, you may:

  • Enable or disable push notifications through your device settings
  • Manage advertising settings through your Android or Google settings
  • Manage applicable advertising consent choices when presented
  • Choose whether to purchase Literary Room Premium

Where required by applicable law, Literary Room and its advertising services may provide appropriate consent mechanisms for advertising-related processing.

11. Third-Party Services

Literary Room uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMobAdvertising and advertising-related services.
  • Firebase Cloud MessagingPush notification delivery.
  • Google Play BillingProcessing of in-app purchases and Premium entitlements.

These services are operated by Google and may be subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our services, applicable laws, or our data practices.

When we make changes, we will update the "Effective Date" at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
