Coloring Book & Learn

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Helping families understand how information is handled.

Privacy first: Coloring Book & Learn does not collect personal information or track children's activity. Optional in-app purchases are processed by the app store.

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Coloring Book & Learn (the “App”), provided by xAssets (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information. The App is designed for children and families.

2. Personal Data and Tracking

We do not collect personal information through the App. The App does not require account registration or ask children to provide their names, email addresses, phone numbers, or location. We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs to track users, and we do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

3. Children's Privacy

We design the App with children's privacy in mind. Children can use its coloring and learning features without submitting personal information to us. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

4. In-App Purchases

The App may offer optional in-app purchases. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store or by Google through Google Play, depending on the platform. We do not receive or store your payment-card details. Apple or Google may process transaction information under their own privacy policies and may provide the App with purchase status or transaction identifiers needed to unlock or restore purchased features.

For more information, please see Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Coloring Artwork and Progress

Coloring artwork, preferences, or progress may be saved locally on your device to support the App's features. We do not upload this content to our servers or collect it for analytics. You can remove locally stored App data using the options available on your device. Please note that uninstalling an app does not always remove cloud backups maintained by your device platform.

6. Device Permissions

If a feature uses a device capability, such as saving an image to the photo library, the operating system may request permission. You can manage permissions in your device settings. Such access is used only to provide the feature you choose; it is not used to collect personal information for us.

7. Third-Party Services

We do not integrate third-party advertising or user-behavior analytics services. The App relies on Apple or Google platform services for distribution and optional in-app purchases. These platforms operate independently and may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Data Retention and Requests

Because we do not collect personal information through the App, we do not maintain a user-profile database to access, correct, or delete. Purchase records held by Apple or Google are managed by those platforms. If you contact us for support, we will use the information you voluntarily provide only to respond to your request and will retain correspondence only as reasonably necessary.

9. Security

We minimize privacy risks by not collecting personal information through the App. In-app payments are handled by the applicable app-store provider using its own security measures.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy if the App's features or data practices change. Any updates will be posted here with a revised “Last updated” date.
