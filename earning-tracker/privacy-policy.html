Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Hourvia (“the App”) is a work-hours and earnings tracking application developed by xAssets (“we,” “us,” or “our”). We designed Hourvia V1 to work locally on your device without requiring an account or a cloud service.

1. Information You Enter

You may enter job details, work sessions, breaks, pay rates, pay rules, and related settings. This information is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this work data.

2. Data Collection and Tracking

Hourvia V1 does not require account registration and does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect your work-session data for analytics or advertising.

3. In-App Purchases

Hourvia offers optional Premium subscriptions and a lifetime purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play, depending on your device. The relevant store handles payment information and may process purchase-related data under its own privacy policy. Hourvia may access purchase status or transaction information made available by the store to determine your Premium entitlement. We do not directly collect or store your payment card details.

4. Offline Use and Internet Access

Core work tracking and local data management are designed to function offline. An internet connection may be required to view store products, complete purchases, restore purchases, or refresh purchase status.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

Your work data is maintained on your device. You can manage or delete records using the features available in the App. Uninstalling the App or losing access to your device may permanently remove locally stored data. Hourvia V1 does not provide an xAssets-operated cloud backup or sync service. Any device-level backup is subject to your operating system and device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Hourvia is intended for people tracking work hours and earnings, and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through an xAssets-operated service.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its practices change. The updated version will display a revised “Last updated” date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact xAssets at xassets.info@gmail.com.
