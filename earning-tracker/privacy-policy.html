Privacy Policy

How EarningTracker handles your work records, purchases, and personal information.

1. Overview

EarningTracker is a work-hours and estimated-earnings tracking app. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app on iOS or Android.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, the app stores job names, work sessions, clock-in and clock-out times, breaks, pay rates, pay periods, earnings estimates, calculation evidence, and app preferences. These records are primarily stored in the app’s private local storage.

3. Local-first operation

The current app does not provide accounts, cloud synchronization, or an app-managed export, backup, or restore service. We do not operate a server that automatically receives your work sessions, pay rates, or earnings records.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

EarningTracker may offer a free tier, an automatically renewing subscription, and a one-time Lifetime Premium purchase. Apple App Store or Google Play processes payments. The app may use store-provided product identifiers, purchase status, transaction identifiers, subscription expiration or renewal status, and revocation information to verify access to Premium features and restore eligible purchases. We do not directly receive your full payment card details.

Billing information and store account data are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. Restoring a purchase restores an eligible entitlement, not locally deleted work records.

5. Analytics and advertising

Based on the current version reviewed for this policy, EarningTracker does not include third-party advertising, behavioral analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. If this changes, we will update this policy and applicable store disclosures before the relevant release.

6. When information may leave your device

Your locally stored work and earnings records are not automatically sent to us. Information may be processed by third parties when you make an in-app purchase, open an external link, use an operating-system backup, or voluntarily contact support. If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send. Please avoid including unnecessary sensitive financial information.

7. Backup and data loss

On Android, the app is configured to disable the automatic app-data backup and device-transfer mechanisms covered by its backup configuration. On iOS, app data may be included in operating-system backups depending on device settings and platform behavior. Neither platform guarantees recovery. Removing the app, losing your device, or storage failure may result in permanent loss of local records. Keep independent records of important work and pay information.

8. Retention and deletion

Local work records generally remain on your device until you modify or delete them using available app functions, or remove the app’s data. Uninstalling the app may delete local records. Any operating-system backup follows the platform’s separate retention rules.

If you contact support, we use your message to respond and handle related requests. Support message retention and deletion procedure: [CONFIRM BEFORE PUBLICATION]. You may request information about support correspondence through the contact below.

9. Security

The app uses private storage provided by iOS and Android. No device or storage mechanism is completely risk-free. Protect access to your device and maintain independent records where needed.

10. Children

EarningTracker is not designed specifically for children and does not knowingly operate a service to collect children’s personal information.

11. International users

The app may be available internationally. Work and earnings records are primarily processed on your device. App stores, backup providers, and email services may process information according to their own terms and applicable laws.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our features, data practices, or legal obligations change. The revised policy will be posted here with a new effective date.
